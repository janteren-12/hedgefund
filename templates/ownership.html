5%+ Ownership Stakes

Schedule 13D and 13G filings, separate from 13F: a fund has to file one whenever it crosses (or materially changes) beneficial ownership of 5% of a single company's stock - unrelated to 13F's $100M-total-portfolio threshold, and unrelated to 10% (a common mix-up, since 10% ownership triggers different rules around insider trading and short-swing profits). 13D means the filer may seek to influence or control the company (the classic activist filing); 13G is the same 5% threshold but declares no such intent. Each row is one filing - a fund with an existing stake that later amends it (buying more, selling down, changing intent) will show multiple rows for the same company, letting you see the stake's history.

Only filings from {{ since }} onward are shown: SEC only required these to be filed in a structured, machine-readable format starting then, and older free-text filings aren't reliably parseable. If nothing shows up below for a fund, it simply hasn't crossed a new 5% threshold or amended an existing one since that date - it doesn't mean the fund holds no concentrated stakes at all. ·
