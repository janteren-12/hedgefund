These groupings are not derived from the 13F data - 13F filings only show holdings, not intent. They're our own labels based on each fund's public reputation, just to make it easier to compare funds with a similar style. Groups and funds are both sorted by 13F portfolio value, biggest first.

Est. Annualized Return is real market data (from the fund's actual public stock/share price) for the rare fund that trades publicly - most hedge funds are private and aren't required to disclose returns anywhere, so those show "Not publicly disclosed" rather than a guessed number. Where a return does show, it's one quarter's real price move compounded up to an annual rate - a genuine number, but based on a single quarter, so treat it as noisy, not a multi-year track record.
