Insider Activity

A different disclosure regime from everywhere else in this app: Form 3/4/5 filings are made by a company's own officers, directors, and 10%+ owners reporting their personal trades in that company's stock - not by any of your tracked hedge funds. Scoped to the top {{ top_n }} stocks your tracked funds hold most widely (the same ranking Overlap uses), covering the last {{ window_days }} days - insider trading is far higher-frequency than 13F/13D/13G, so this is a rolling window, not full history.

Only trades in actual common stock are shown, not option/RSU activity. Every transaction is labeled with what it actually is - most Form 4s are routine compensation events (grants, tax withholding, option exercises), not a genuine market decision, so "insider sold" isn't always what it sounds like.
