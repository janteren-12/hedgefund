Overlap: Who Holds What

Each row is a stock held by 2 or more tracked funds. Each column is a fund, ordered biggest to smallest by 13F portfolio value (shown under the fund name) - not full AUM, since 13F only covers long U.S. equity positions. The number in a fund's column is that stock's % of that fund's 13F portfolio. A blank cell means that fund doesn't hold the stock. Weighted Avg % is the average of the holding funds' percentages, weighted by each fund's own size - a signal of how much collective conviction these funds have, not investment advice (it ignores valuation, correlation between the funds' bets, and that 13F data can be up to 45 days stale).
