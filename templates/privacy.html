Privacy & Disclaimer

Privacy

This app doesn't collect, store, or share any personal information about you. There are no user accounts, no cookies, and no analytics or third-party trackers of any kind.

If this app is deployed on a hosting service (e.g. Vercel), that service may keep its own standard infrastructure logs (such as request IP addresses) as part of running any website - that's the hosting provider's own logging, not something this app adds, reads, or controls.

Data & Disclaimer

Every number in this app comes from public filings on SEC EDGAR - 13F-HR quarterly holdings reports and Schedule 13D/13G beneficial ownership filings. Nothing here is scraped from a paywalled source or independently verified beyond the parsing logic described in this project's README.

This is not investment advice. The underlying data is inherently delayed by design: 13F filings can lag up to 45 days after each quarter-end, and 13D/13G filings only appear when a fund crosses (or materially changes) a 5% ownership threshold in some company - not in real time. 13F also only covers long U.S. equity positions, not a fund's full portfolio (no shorts, cash, bonds, derivatives, or non-U.S. holdings).
