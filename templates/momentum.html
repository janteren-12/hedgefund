Momentum: Who's Buying, Who's Selling

Same grid as Overlap, but each cell is the change in a fund's portfolio weight in a stock between its two most recent stored filings, not the weight itself. +10.0% means that fund added roughly 10 percentage points of its own portfolio to the stock; -10.0% means it trimmed by roughly that much. NEW marks a brand-new position, SOLD a full exit. Only stocks where at least one fund's share count actually changed are shown - so unlike Overlap, a blank cell can mean either "doesn't hold it" or "holds it but didn't trade it," and a stock with no changes anywhere doesn't appear at all. The percentage itself is based on portfolio value, so it can be nudged by the stock's own price move as well as by actual buying or selling - the row only appears because shares changed, but the size of the move shown isn't pure trading.
